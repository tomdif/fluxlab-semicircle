CASE 1 ONLY · IDENTICAL INITIAL CONDITIONS

Follow the field. Then the particles.

Field-line cross-section

Poincaré intersections at toroidal angle φ = 0. Six starts, about 25–26 toroidal returns each.

Field lines describe magnetic topology. They are not particle trajectories. Integration uses the direct Biot–Savart field.

24 test deuterons · 1 keV · 100 µs

Collisionless vacuum guiding centers, seeded positions and pitches. No plasma self-field, heating or collisions.

Maximum cross-sectional distance from the reference axis, not a flux-coordinate radius or confinement time.

The counting boundary is a numerical observation envelope (0.55 < R < 1.50 m, |Z| < 0.40 m), not a last closed flux surface or reactor wall. These short, small ensembles do not establish a confinement gain.
